Free registration

Zero joining fee and no mandatory starter kit. Anyone can register as a customer at no cost.

🪪

KYC before payout

PAN, Aadhaar and bank-account verification are required before any commission is released.

📦

100% product-linked BV

BV is generated only on product orders. No income is paid for recruitment alone.

⏳

7-day return escrow

Order BV stays in Pending_Escrow for 7 days after delivery. Returns automatically reverse BV and commissions.

↩️

30-day buyback

Unopened, marketable products returned within 30 days of invoice are refunded, less applicable taxes and clawback of paid BV commissions.

🧢

70% hard cap

Total payout across all streams can never exceed 70% of total BV in any cycle. The engine enforces this automatically.

Anti-leakage payout engine

5% Cashback

+ 20% Effective Binary (40% on weaker leg)
+ 20% 6-Level Generation
+ 5% Effective Leadership Match

TruePoints logic: 5,000 steps = 10 TruePoints; 1 TruePoint = ₹1 discount, capped at 10% of the bill. BV is calculated strictly on net INR paid via UPI/Card/NetBanking after the TruePoints deduction.
